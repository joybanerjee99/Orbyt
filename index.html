how orbyt works

Orbyt detects when you or a contact are travelling — in a city that isn't your home town, or at an airport. When you're both in the same place, you each get an Orbyt Notice and can message each other directly in the app. Your exact location is never visible to anyone. Contacts can only detect you if you have each other in your lists.
